# Founder Checklist for Startup Credit Applications

Do not commit the answers to this checklist. Enter them only in the provider's official form after reviewing the prepared application text.

## Identity and contact

- Confirm the Latin spelling of the founder's full legal name exactly as shown on the identity document used for verification.
- Use a stable personal email controlled by the founder inside application forms. Do not publish it as a support address; add a domain email later if needed.
- Provide a phone number that can receive account-verification messages.

## Country, address, and billing

- For each provider, select a country and billing address that truthfully match the payment card and documents used for that account.
- Do not describe SkillCue as a Vietnamese company unless a Vietnamese legal entity is actually registered.
- Confirm how the provider wants the Russian self-employed status represented; do not translate it as a corporation.

## Program history

- Confirm that SkillCue and the founder have not previously received startup credits from the same provider.
- Confirm that there is no investor, accelerator, or venture-fund referral code before selecting a self-funded tier.
- Confirm that the application does not claim users, revenue, funding, partnerships, or previous grants.

## Public proof

- Verify that <https://skill-cue.ru/> is available.
- Verify that the English overview at <https://skill-cue.ru/en/> is available.
- Verify that the latest Windows installer downloads successfully.
- Review the public GitHub showcase after it is updated and before submitting any form.
- Confirm that the Telegram support account [@SkillCue](https://t.me/SkillCue) is monitored.

## Final review before submission

- Read every legal-status and residency statement.
- Check the requested tier and provider services.
- Check that the credit-use plan matches the intended architecture.
- Review any terms that may cause automatic pay-as-you-go billing after credits expire.
- Save a private copy of the submitted answers and confirmation email outside the code repository.

## Never put in the repository

- Passport or identity-document numbers.
- Residential address.
- Tax identifiers beyond information already intentionally published in legal notices.
- Card number, CVV, bank details, passwords, one-time codes, or API keys.
- Screenshots of provider portals containing account identifiers or balances.
