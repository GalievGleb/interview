Product facts

Transparent about data and stage.

SkillCue is a pre-launch MVP built for Russian-speaking job seekers. The commercial source code remains private; the public repository contains product documentation and Windows releases.

Cloud speech recognitionCompleted audio fragments are sent to OpenAI directly or through the SkillCue gateway for transcription.
Local preparation data by defaultVacancies, resumes, and preparation history stay on the user's device unless a requested cloud feature needs specific text.

Responsible use

Practice your experience. Do not invent it.

SkillCue does not endorse fabricated employment history, impersonation, or violation of an employer's interview rules. Candidates remain responsible for the accuracy of their answers and how they use supporting materials.
